## Boundary
The **NVE-D environment itself** is modeled as a finite IETF-inspired substrate of isolated test nodes, immutable source snapshots, finite experimental clocks, checkpoint/resume and evidence aggregation. Each node has a constant kernel binding `K_ENV := K_candidate`, corresponding to the *candidate* `|x|` from prior trials. The supplied `|#|`, `#`, `::`, `:`, `(?)` and relative positions are retained **as an opaque proposed governing rule**, without asserting operator, separator, variable or audit meanings. A runnable interpreter cannot be justified until alternative candidate interpretations and observable tests are specified.

## Locally executed infrastructure probe
Python fixture used three deliberately artificial kernels (identity, reverse, rotate) on three opaque source expressions, with two seeds and four generation budgets (250, 1,000, 2,500, 5,000). **24 independent runs, 52,500 completed generations**. Constant kernel identity held and all checkpoint replay states matched. These `=` labels refer to explicit fixture equality only. No symbolic semantics, kernel discovery, meta-specification, or IETF infinity demonstrated.
