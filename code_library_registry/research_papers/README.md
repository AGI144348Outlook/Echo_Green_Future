# Research Papers Registry

This section preserves non-code research papers, essays, conceptual analyses, and governance/ethics documents that form part of Echo/EVE's intellectual lineage.

## Sections
- **Ethics & Governance** — normative frameworks, research ethics, rights, stewardship, safety, and governance.
- **Architecture & Strategy** — architectural synthesis, platform concepts, technical strategy, product strategy, and historical market analysis.
- Future research-paper categories may be added without mixing papers into the code-artifact namespace.

## Identifier convention
Research papers use `RP-XXXX` identifiers independently of `CLR-XXXX` code/library artifacts.

## Epistemic handling
Archival preservation does not imply endorsement or validation. Assessments should distinguish argument, hypothesis, factual premise, cited evidence, and open research question.
