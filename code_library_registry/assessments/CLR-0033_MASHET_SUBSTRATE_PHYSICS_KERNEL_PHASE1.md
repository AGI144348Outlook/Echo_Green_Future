## Critical audit notes
1. The semantic/frequency assignments remain authored project mappings; numerical execution does not independently validate their Hebrew or semantic meaning.
2. “No lookup tables” is contradicted by _KW_BOOST, _PRIM_FREQS, letter tags, and explicit registries.
3. Recursive/field behavior should not be called fractal, consciousness, or emergence without corresponding measurements.
4. Lipschitz guarding is a real numerical constraint mechanism, but L<1 on each guarded update does not by itself prove global stability of the complete stochastic/stateful system.
5. Random noise means reproducibility requires seeded RNG and run metadata.
6. Resonance thresholding can generate a traversal, but threshold crossing does not establish linguistic validity or semantic grounding.
7. Indus labels/meanings are project assignments unless separately sourced/validated.
8. Several letters have no direct operator function; their behavior comes through frequency/tag/field placement rather than an executable glyph-specific operator. Preserve that distinction.
9. Keyword substring matching remains vulnerable to accidental matches.
