## Executive assessment
This artifact extends the EVE conceptual architecture into a whole-body humanoid avatar specification. It catalogs genetic, cellular, cardiovascular, respiratory, nervous, sensory, endocrine, digestive, immune, reproductive, metabolic, and homeostatic systems, then connects those systems to the historical Dual-LVT consciousness model.

Its strongest current value is **hierarchical systems decomposition**: avatar → organ systems → subsystems → mechanisms → state/control relationships. That pattern is potentially useful for Echo's environment/entity modeling and registries.

The Planck-unit construction, consciousness-driven morphology, digital-biological unification, and claimed 28-year incarnation testing are not established scientific results and must remain classified as conceptual or unverified provenance.

## Scientific/medical boundary
Many ordinary anatomy and physiology descriptions are broadly recognizable, but the document mixes them with inaccurate, outdated, oversimplified, or unsupported claims. It should therefore not be imported wholesale as a validated biomedical knowledge base.

Examples requiring correction/qualification:
- A 70 kg human is not meaningfully described as "~7 × 10^27 Planck units"; Planck length is a length scale, not a unit of mass or known biological constituent.
- "Each cell contains ~10^12 Planck units" is not physically meaningful under standard physics.
- "Individual atom positioning within 1 Planck length" is not a demonstrated biological construction capability.
- Human genomic counts depend on whether one means haploid vs diploid and what is being counted; the supplied "3.2 billion bp (diploid human genome)" wording conflates common genome-size conventions.
- Textbook ATP yield is not simply "~38 ATP per glucose" for human cells; modern estimates are typically lower and context-dependent.
- Many physiological quantities are approximate and vary across individuals and measurement conventions.
- Conscious intent does not provide demonstrated arbitrary real-time control of cellular morphology, tissue regeneration, sensory sensitivity, or evolutionary adaptation.

These should be retained as historical source statements with epistemic tags rather than silently normalized as facts.

## Validation/provenance issue
The artifact states:
