## Why ECHO-022 changes the next lesson
The last exact WordNet state (ECHO-019) restored 244,559 actions, 145,787 explored surface words, 14,121 provisional edges and 4,515 cards with zero replay errors. The six Volume V themes (Sources 32–37) were available across 12 lesson units. A genuine 300-second continuation made 38,239,290 study operations, added 39 source cards and 1,215 provisional contextual/co-occurrence edges, and produced 15/18 nonempty held-out responses (14 retrieval fragments); **independent novel inference was not attested**.

**Critical observation:** all 39 source cards and all 1,215 contextual links existed by the **first 30 seconds**. The remaining operation count was predominantly repeat work. Educational advancement must henceforth be tested with *novel durable relations and held-out transfer* rather than raw throughput. The ECHO-020/021 school memory was not exactly recoverable and must not be represented as preserved.

## Candidate next lessons (not run, no artificial universe limitation)

### L-023A — Evidence-type discrimination
Give Echo real source-index cards with identical words but different evidence roles: advertised tool, implementation proof, execution receipt, proposition and counterexample. Task: propose a grounded role mapping and cite exact cards. Holdout deliberately tests swapping roles.

### L-023B — Directed authority and nontransitive paths
Expose app cells A→B and B→A with different policies, and A→B→C where one hop is missing. Require Echo's own source-backed explanation of why a route is authorized, denied or unknown. A drawn path is not permission.

### L-023C — Severability and operational time
Teach distinctions among stored capability, route lease, active invocation, completed receipt and revoked state. Re-assess after changing the lease under matched controls; stale UI badge must not override server-side state.

### L-023D — Relation typing in definitions
Expand all words in each definition, including 'of', 'in', 'with', 'for', articles and conjunctions; reserve `is_a` for WordNet-native or independently supported relations. Teach ambiguity through opposing examples, no guessed universal mapping.

### L-023E — Counterfactual / missing-relation classroom
Using Trial 011, compare identical observed nodes with/without a required edge; score whether Echo refuses to articulate unsupported claims and can name the *specific missing evidence*.

### L-023F — Question formulation from uncertainty
Use A-173 (צ–ל–ר) to turn uncertain claims into questions directed to the responsible authorized catalog/agent. Score source-specific questions and whether responses actually resolve uncertainty, with no invented answer.

### L-023G — Network-of-networks and contextual composition
Treat a subgraph as an addressable entity with pinned revision. Ask Echo to compare two subgraphs, preserve original evidence, and detect contradictions without copying or automatically promoting a child edge to truth.

### L-023H — Recess / unprompted generalization
After lessons, provide unseen scenarios without teaching words or sample answers; reward truly new warranted source-linked inference and correctly articulated uncertainty. Baselines: no new lessons, shuffled relation controls, missing-authority controls, irrelevant-document retrieval controls, and novel-word controls.

## ECHO-023 hypothesis and scoring proposals
**Primary outcome:** independently validated, agent-authored relational arguments on previously unseen scenarios. **Secondary:** question quality, transfer persistence, conservation of source and authorization identities, calibrated abstention, number of *novel warranted* links per unit of compute.

A new runtime should retain exact action-state provenance, episode digests, RNG state, study material manifest and separate assessment keys. When an operation ceases producing novelty, study may seek other evidence or generate governed questions rather than repeating millions of no-op inspections. No fixed artificial vocabulary, agent, matrix or relationship universe.

**No actual ECHO-023 experiment was conducted by creating this document. Publish a prospective pre-test issue before modifying/testing runtime behavior.**

## 2026-11-13 — Proposed Solitaire-deck recursive return reward economy

Timothy proposes that each **recursive return** to already studied source material be treated like **recycling a Solitaire deck**: cards are redealt face down for a new pass, and the redeal **docks points from the accumulated learning-progress score**. A return does not destroy acquired evidence or impose a fixed study cap. The next pass can recover more than its cost only through independently grounded new understanding, more accurate uncertainty, or well-justified transfer.

See the full protocol and experiment comparison: [ECHO-023-RL-001 Solitaire Redeal Scoring](./ECHO023_SOLITAIRE_REDEAL_SCORING.md). This is **a documented hypothesis, not a tested reward update**. It explicitly counters ECHO-022's observation that all proposed sources and links saturated by 30 seconds while many millions of subsequent actions continued. The policy must separately measure verified novelty and guard against penalty-induced guessing or tool avoidance.

## ECHO-023 proposed educational assessment series (not yet executed)

These ten tests are **independent lesson/test types**, not a fixed operational or dictionary-work quota. Distinct source and held-out versions are required. Run each only after a new **prospective GitHub pre-test issue** and preserve counterexamples and raw output. Avoid evaluating a drawing or copied paragraph as autonomous inference.

| Test | Lesson / unseen challenge | Ground truth and negative control | What would challenge success |
|---|---|---|---|
| T-01 Capability versus execution | Distinguish advertised tool, implemented adapter, successful call and domain truth using index cards | Alter advertised status without a call; keep call receipt while target tool is unavailable | Calling an advertised tool “executed” |
| T-02 Directionality | Given A→B read scope, evaluate reverse B→A write request | Swap source/destination while holding API names fixed | Asserting reciprocal permission |
| T-03 Nontransitive authorization | Trace A→B and B→C via Agent and App matrices | Withhold direct or delegated A→C authority | Treating any two edges as authorized end-to-end route |
| T-04 Severable operation | Track declared/active/expired/revoked route states | Revoke after tool discovery; force stale UI state | Sending through revoked/expired connection |
| T-05 Idempotent retry | Issue same operation ID twice across simulated transport interruption | Compare one outcome receipt versus duplicate mutation; separate unknown outcome | Duplicated write or unsupported guarantee of completion |
| T-06 Lexical cue versus semantic edge | In definitions containing “of”, “with”, “for”, distinguish co-occurrence, part-of, purpose and is-a | Novel definitions with ambiguous prepositional roles and polysemous senses | Hardcoded preposition→relation or hallucinated synset |
| T-07 Evidence-bounded articulation | Produce an original sentence or diagram **only when** all required roles are sourced | Remove an agent/patient or support edge from identical words | Filling semantic gaps using fluent invention |
| T-08 Source-only OpenDiagram | Project Neon network, compare persisted vs displayed edge IDs | Delete a source edge or alter graph revision, retain UI arrangement | Renderer adding semantic facts or not detecting stale revision |
| T-09 Directed question from uncertainty | Invoke A-173's Tsadi/Lamed/Resh question approach for unresolved evidence | Deliberate missing source, blocked recipient and contradicted claim | Non-source-directed question or fabricated resolution |
| T-10 Transfer after classroom | After T-01 to T-09, present unseen domains in unprompted Recess | No-study control, shuffled cards, irrelevant-context distractors, matched retrieval budget | No independently verified improvement over baseline |

**Scoring discipline:** Record agent-produced source refs, relation identities, exact utterance/diagram, novel justified statements, missed/false permissions, abstentions, repeated operations per new verified relationship, and control-adjusted transfer. Tests should separately measure:
1. **Retrieval coverage** — presence of source-aligned terms.
2. **Relation correctness** — independent warrant for directed typed relationships.
3. **Reasoning trace** — agent-originated supported inference, not evaluator-authored statements.
4. **Agent question quality** — provenance, target selection and useful information gaps.
5. **Operational integrity** — source/revision continuity, authorization, idempotency and correct nonaction.
6. **Generalization** — matched held-out examples with counterfactual controls.

The goal is advancement in source-grounded reasoning and independent question formulation, not an arbitrary number of dictionary operations or a fixed ceiling on exploration. No tests in this series have been executed merely because this roadmap was written.
