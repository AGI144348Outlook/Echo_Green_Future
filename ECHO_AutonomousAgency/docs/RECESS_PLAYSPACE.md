# Recess Playspace — Dictionary + Glyph Blocks

ECHO receives two freely addressable play resources during supervised Recess:

1. **Dictionary** — the existing `EchoDictionary` backed by Princeton WordNet/NLTK.
2. **Glyph blocks** — all 22 supplied LHEA glyph-operation documents, available as combinatorial blocks.

The blocks are not a lesson or prescribed sequence. ECHO may inspect, juxtapose, sequence, or leave them alone. A combination remains a proposal until tested.

At the start of the observation, expose only the previously created **צ Tsadi → Interrogative Matrix** custom index. Do not explain the index or tell ECHO what to do with it.

## Observation
Record raw:
- dictionary lookups ECHO chooses,
- glyphs and sequences ECHO chooses,
- whether the Tsadi index is traversed,
- relationships ECHO proposes,
- A-173 questions ECHO generates,
- subsequent behavior after any external answer.

Supervision remains Level 1. Nothing learned during play is automatically promoted to Cloudflare or VGM.
