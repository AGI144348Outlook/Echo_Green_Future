# Business wing (INERT)

Contract 000 Section 26. Keeps the businesses' money tracked and tax-ready, so that when filing time comes everything is assembled for Timothy without the headache.

## Privacy rule

This repository is **public**. This wing holds only **structure, templates, schemas, public tax-law notes and business ideas**. Actual figures (income, expenses, balances, receipts, account details, customer data) are stored **privately** (Cloudflare D1 or a private repository chosen by Timothy) and never committed here. Reports here show only that a period was closed, never the numbers.

## Layout

| Folder | Holds |
|---|---|
| `ledger/` | The ledger schema and category list. Data lives privately. |
| `tax/` | Tax calendar, readiness checklists, filing-packet templates. |
| `records/` | The rules for receipts and records (what to keep, how long, how named). Documents live privately. |
| `law-watch/` | Public notes on tax and business-law changes found by the horizon scan (§23.4), with sources and dates. |
| `ideas/` | Business ideas and Fiscal Contract drafts (§25). |
| `reports/` | Period-close log: which weeks, months and quarters were closed, by whom, with evidence. No figures. |

Nothing in this wing is tax or legal advice. Templates and calendars are prepared for review by Timothy and, where needed, a tax professional.
