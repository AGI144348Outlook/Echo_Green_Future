# Records rules (draft)

- Every income and expense row has a `receipt_ref` to a stored source document (processor statement, invoice, receipt).
- Documents are stored privately, named `YYYY-MM-DD_<source>_<ref>`.
- Keep records at least as long as the tax authority can review a return (commonly several years; confirm the current period with a tax professional).
- Never store full card or bank account numbers anywhere in records.
