# Tax readiness calendar (draft; confirm dates each year)

| When | Readiness task |
|---|---|
| **Weekly** | All transactions entered and categorized; missing receipts flagged |
| **Monthly** | Month closed: income statement and balance sheet produced privately; runway and coverage ratio (§25.4) updated |
| **Quarterly** | Estimated-tax worksheet prepared for Timothy's review before each federal estimated-payment deadline (traditionally mid-April, mid-June, mid-September and mid-January) |
| **January** | Collect year-end forms from processors and platforms (for example 1099-K or 1099-NEC where issued) |
| **Year-end packet** | Full-year income statement, expense totals by category, asset list, mileage or home-office records if any, processor statements, and an index of receipts, ready to hand to a preparer or file |
| **Ongoing** | Law-watch entries reviewed; any change that affects filing is flagged `awaiting:timothy` |

State notes to research and confirm: Texas has no personal state income tax, but business entities and sales of some digital products and services may carry Texas obligations (franchise tax, sales tax). The horizon scan confirms the current rules before any business takes its first sale.
