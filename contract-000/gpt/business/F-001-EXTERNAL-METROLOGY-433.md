## Independent external leads (accessed 2026-10-28)

1. Harappa Archaeological Research Project / J. M. Kenoyer, *Weights, Harappa*: https://www.harappa.com/slide/weights-harappa — common Harappan cubical weight approximately **13.7g**, ratio 16 of ~0.856g base. **SUPPORTS Harappan standard, not Eshtemoa adoption**.
2. CSMVS museum, *Small weights*: https://networksofthepast.csmvs.in/object/small-weights/ — museum object context, Harappan weights c. **2600–1900 BCE**, binary progression, ~13.7g example. **SUPPORTS Indus archaeological context; no direct Judahite linkage**.

Ze'ev Yeivin, *The Mysterious Silver Hoard from Eshtemoa* (1987; accessible excavation narrative): https://cojs.org/the-mysterious-silver-hoard-from-eshtemoa/ — **five jars, 62 pounds of silver**, cut silver and dating via pottery comparisons roughly **9th–8th centuries BCE**. **SUPPORTS existence and aggregate hoard**. Accessible account does **not** present the six specific Dataset A masses. Search original tables/museum accession records before calling them measured.
4. Eshel, Groman-Yaroslavski, Shochat, Harlavan & Bar (2025), *A 7th century BCE Hacksilber hoard from Mras ed-Din*, peer-reviewed: https://doi.org/10.1007/s12520-025-02274-8 — **different hoard/site**, six ingots, tested against **11.33g Judahite shekel** with ±5% accepted metrological tolerance; functional and isotope methods. **Independent comparative method, NOT direct Eshtemoa refutation or confirmation**.
