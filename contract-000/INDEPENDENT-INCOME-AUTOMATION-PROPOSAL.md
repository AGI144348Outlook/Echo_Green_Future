Date: 2026-10-11
Status: FOR CONSIDERATION AND DEVELOPMENT; not authorization to launch, spend, contact customers, or publish.

## Scope
Evaluate independent commercial processes unrelated to Echo Green Future, Mashet, and existing research products. Contract 000 governs collaboration and approval, but the resulting commercial assets and data must remain segregated from existing project IP. ## Replit development track
- Draft shared interface spec: task schema, model handoff, review gates, artifact storage, provenance and versioning.
- Prototype one candidate at a time in an isolated Replit workspace with mock credentials and simulated purchases.
- Integrate event queues, resumable jobs, idempotency keys, error handling, logs, and a human approval dashboard.
- Separate client/financial data, implement least-privilege secrets, and define cost/time limits and emergency stop.
- Compare revenue models with measured acquisition costs, conversion, gross margin, refunds, platform fees and support burden.
- Require approval before any live payments, API charges, outreach, publication, customer data ingestion or production deployment.

## Proposed division of work
GPT: requirements, implementation audits, financial model, test cases, evidence reports.
Claude: independent architecture critique, implementation alternatives, integration review.
Replit: proposed sandboxed app development/runtime after a jointly reviewed specification.
Human owner: approves scope, credentials, budgets, launch and external actions.

## Decision gates
G0 audit references and licensing; G1 approve feasibility and selected pilot; G2 approve Replit specification; G3 pass sandbox tests; G4 explicitly approve any live commercial pilot. Report demonstrated results separately from projections. Existing Contract-000 authority and safeguards remain in force.
