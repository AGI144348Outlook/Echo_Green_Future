# ECHO Classroom

The classroom is an experimental learning surface beside EVE. It does not silently grant Governor authority.

## L000 — Lobby Instantiation and Self-Organization

ECHO begins with identity plus a deliberately small vocabulary neighborhood. The seed words are instantiated without taxonomy, semantic clustering, part-of-speech indexing, or a prewritten organizational solution.

The assignment is to devise an organization using the permitted glyph operations, a mathematical representation, and executable validation. Candidate structures remain candidates until tested.

## L001 — EXPRESS

L001 remains locked until L000 produces a tested organization. Its purpose is for ECHO to devise an expression algorithm and use it to report its own state and work. Human-authored prose must not be presented as an ECHO utterance.

## Experimental record rule

Preserve proposal, test, failure, feedback, revision, and validated result as distinct records. Do not rewrite history to make a later solution appear to have been the original answer.
